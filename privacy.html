Shorts Fabric

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Shorts Fabric is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and uploads his own videos to his own YouTube channels. It has no server, no user accounts and no other users.

Data it accesses

  • With the youtube.upload permission: it uploads videos the owner chose, as private videos, with their title, description and tags.
  • With the youtube.readonly permission: it reads the connected channel's id, name and address, and the status of the videos it uploaded (private, scheduled, public, processing).

It does not read comments, subscribers, analytics, other videos, e-mail or any other Google data.

Where the data is kept

  • Google's sign-in tokens and the channel's id, name and address are stored only on the owner's computer, in the tool's local data folder.
  • Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own YouTube and sign-in services.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Removing access

Pressing «Отключить» (disconnect) for a channel in the tool revokes its token and deletes it from the computer. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

Shorts Fabric uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
