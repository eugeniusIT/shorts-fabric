Shorts Fabric

A personal desktop tool that makes short illustrated videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Runs only on its owner's computer. It is not a public service, and nobody else signs in to it.
  • Uploads a finished video to a channel the owner connected, always as a private video, with its title, description and tags. A publish time can be set at upload.
  • Reads back the channel's name and the uploaded video's status, so the owner sees whether it is still private, scheduled or public.
  • Never edits, publishes or deletes videos already on YouTube. The owner publishes each video himself in YouTube Studio.

Google access it asks for

  • youtube.upload: to upload the owner's videos.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the channel's name and the status of the videos it uploaded.

Privacy policy
